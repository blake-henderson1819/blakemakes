## Preface

Last summer I went down to San Diego for my internship, and what do people love to do most in San Diego? Surf. I loved surfing there too, until I started getting shoulder pain from paddling for hours a day, several times a week.

While sitting on the water waiting for a set to come in, a dumb, slightly devious idea dawned on me: what if I built a remote-controlled surfboard powered by a LiPo battery? My better judgement told me it was a stupid idea that wouldn't actually work, but curiosity won out. I imagined how funny it would be to cruise past my fellow surfers without lifting a finger.

So I got to work right away: I CADed a waterproof enclosure, made a bill of materials, and ordered the parts: the speed controller, the propeller, a gasket seal, cable glands, and a wireless RC receiver and remote. This is what I made:
